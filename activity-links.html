How this directory works

Choose the type of study support you need

The same core tools are available for the lower limb, upper limb, and axial regions; the anatomical structures change with the region you select.

Interactive practice

Today’s Practice adapts to progress saved in this browser, Quick 10 provides a short mixed set, and Region Sprint narrows practice to one anatomical area. Other links emphasize flashcards, image identification, labeling, commonly confused structures, or practical-style recall.

Focused drills

Use these for targeted repetition in reverse recall, actions, retrieval, left/right orientation, sticker identification, or muscle OIAs. OIA means origin, insertion, and action.

Reference and review

Open structure lists, muscle image banks, bone and marking tables, origin-insertion-action references, visual libraries, comparison guides, and condensed final reviews.
