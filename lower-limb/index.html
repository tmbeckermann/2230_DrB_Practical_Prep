Bones and Markings Checklist

What this isThe course checklist of lower-limb bones and markings students may see labeled on physical models.
What to knowBe able to answer a sticker-style prompt such as "What is the marking labeled 2B?" and identify left versus right when relevant.
How to use itFilter by region, mark items as done, use Visuals to stretch limited lab time, and confirm with physical models in lab.

Common traps

  • Condyle and epicondyle are not interchangeable; epicondyles sit above condyles.
  • Intertrochanteric line is anterior; intertrochanteric crest is posterior.
  • Medial malleolus belongs to tibia; lateral malleolus belongs to fibula.
  • The fibular apex, or styloid process, is posterolateral on the fibular head.
  • For L/R practice, identify the view first, then decide side.

Muscles to ID

Match practical model IDs to muscles with clearly labeled image sources.
What this isThe muscle-ID list for the single-leg teaching model.
What to knowIdentify each tested muscle on that single-leg model.
How to use itStart with the existing course model views, then use highlighted PAL substitutes for single-target recall.

Course model views are existing multi-label reference material. PAL atlas substitutes are not photographs of Belmont lab models.

Muscle OIAs

What this isThe course origin, insertion, and action table for lower-limb muscles.
What to knowBe able to answer prompts such as "Identify the origin, insertion, and action of the Sartorius" using a bank of OIA choices.
How to use itUse the muscle-group dropdown, study the landmark links, then test recall with OIA Reverse Recall and Action Lookup.
