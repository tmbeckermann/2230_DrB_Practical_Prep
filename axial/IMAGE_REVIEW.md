# Axial skeleton image review

## Integration summary

- 64 paired labeled/unlabeled atlas views were retained for the visual library.
- 168 supplied highlights were mapped to 58 BIO 2230 course terms.
- 263 highlights were excluded from single-answer drills because they were outside the current course term list or did not name the course structure precisely enough.
- Four isolated left/right source views (two temporal-bone and two maxilla views) generate eight orientation cards.

## Course images still needed

The supplied set does not contain a direct, unambiguous highlight for these six course terms:

1. Frontal Bone — Supraorbital margin
2. Frontal Bone — Medial margin of orbital rim
3. Nasal Bones — Paired left and right nasal bones
4. Mandible — Mandibular symphysis
5. Vertebral Types — Sacral vertebra
6. Vertebral Markings — Superior costal facets

Related source images were not substituted when they could teach the wrong scope. For example, the supplied nasal-bone highlights identify one bone rather than the paired bones, the sacrum images show the fused sacrum rather than one sacral vertebra, and the thoracic-vertebra source uses generic “costal facet” or “costal demifacet” labels rather than explicitly identifying the superior costal facet.

## Existing images that would benefit from improvement

- **A2073 and A2074 rib views:** The rib occupies a relatively small part of the frame. A tighter crop and larger landmark callouts would make the head, neck, tubercle/facet, angle, and groove easier to study.
- **A3316 and A3317 thoracic-cage views:** These are useful context plates, but the label density is high at study-card size. Add separate sternum, true/false/floating-rib, and upper/lower cage crops.
- **A2031a, A2031b, A2043a, and A2043b cranial-cavity views:** Small foramina and canals compete with the broad skull-base view. Higher-contrast close-ups or magnified insets would improve rotundum/ovale/spinosum, the acoustic canals, carotid canal, and jugular foramen.
- **A3341 and A3342 full vertebral-column views:** Keep these as regional context, but add cervical, thoracic, lumbar, sacral, and coccygeal crops for practical-style identification.

The isolated skull, sternum, atlas/axis, cervical/thoracic/lumbar vertebra, mandible, maxilla, and major suture views are clear enough for the current drills.
