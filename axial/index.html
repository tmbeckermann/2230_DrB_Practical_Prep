Bones and Markings Checklist

What this isThe course checklist of axial bones and markings students may see labeled on skull, vertebral, and thoracic models.
What to knowBe able to answer a sticker-style prompt such as "What is the marking labeled 2B?" and identify left versus right when relevant.
How to use itFilter by region, mark items as done, use Visuals to stretch limited lab time, and confirm with physical models in lab.

Common traps

  • Pair every skull marking with its correct bone before memorizing its position.
  • Cervical vertebrae have transverse foramina; thoracic vertebrae have costal facets.
  • The dens identifies the axis (C2); the atlas (C1) lacks a typical vertebral body.
  • Distinguish mandibular condylar and coronoid processes before identifying the notch between them.
  • For L/R practice, identify the view first, then decide side.

Practical-Style Labeling

Blank leader-line images for practical readiness. There is no word bank on this page.

What this isA no-word-bank check using the same kind of blank labeling images students see during practical prep.
What to knowRecall the structure names from memory and confirm only after you have committed to an answer.
How to use itChoose an image set, say or write every label first, then reveal the labeled answer and review misses in Visuals or the word-bank page.

Muscles to ID

Match practical model IDs to muscles with clearly labeled image sources.
What this isImage identification for facial muscles plus limited torso-reference practice for other axial muscles.
What to knowFacial and mastication muscles are tested from an image, not a physical face model.
How to use itUse face images for facial muscles. Use torso-focused PAL references only for items that are absent from the tested single-leg and arm models.

The facial course image and PAL atlas substitutes are not claimed as course lab-model photographs.

Muscle OIAs

What this isThe course origin, insertion, and action table for axial muscles.
What to knowBe able to answer prompts such as "Identify the origin, insertion, and action of the masseter" using a bank of OIA choices.
How to use itUse the muscle-group dropdown, study the landmark links, then test recall with OIA Reverse Recall and Action Lookup.

Visual Library

Verified highlighted views and labeled references.

What this isA verified image library of highlighted structures and labeled reference views.
What to knowRecognize the highlighted bone, marking, or muscle from more than one view when multiple images are shown.
How to use itUse quick links for nearby cards, open full-size images when needed, and shuffle to avoid memorizing the order.

Practical Mode

Mixed practical readiness check with no word bank and no review list during the run.

What this isA mixed readiness check that pulls from sticker-style prompts, left/right ID, model muscle ID, and OIA recall. Left/right items are scored automatically; other items are self-checked after results appear.
What to knowCommit to an answer without seeing the key, then compare after the full run is complete.
How to use itChoose the item types and question count, submit each item, then use the revealed results links to review misses.
